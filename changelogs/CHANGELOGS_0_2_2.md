# Strainworks 0.2.2

Release date: 2026-11-25.

The endgame after hatching, and the fixes from the next play test: room to breathe between swarms, a stage 2 that
asks for a fight instead of a sorter, a sorter that takes orders from the nerves like a gate.

## The endgame on the planet

- Hatching a strain purifies its hexagon for good and opens its neighbours: the next station is founded beside
  one already purified. An older planet is brought over once, and a planet file carries it whole.
- Each hatching offers three strain mutations drawn from a designed pool; the one kept applies to every later
  station on that planet, each with its ceiling.
- Rings: the farther a hexagon lies from the first purified one, the harder it is: one more creature family a
  ring, swarms a quarter bigger and deposits a tenth poorer, up to the third ring. The planet shows each open
  hexagon's ring.
- The grand victory: purify a region, one hexagon and all its neighbours (seven, or six round a pentagon). The
  planet shows the region's progress and marks the missing hexagons; free mode can neither give nor take it.

## Concept cards

- A rule met for the first time opens a card in the same window as a building's: a looping scene drawn with the
  game's own pieces, two lines and the key figures, shown once and replayed from the help under Concepts.
- Cards for the base genes and the core's hunger, the swarms and their alarm, the nests that guard rich deposits
  and what they pay, the stage's mechanic, the cap of three harvesters on a deposit and an encapsulator set on the
  wrong box, beside the planet's own: purification, strain mutation, rings and the grand victory.

## Play test fixes

- Swarms are spaced out: once one is cleared (or three minutes after it landed) a quiet minute passes before the
  next is announced, three minutes ahead, so two are at least four minutes apart instead of two. They grow by one
  creature every eight minutes instead of four.
- A swarm is announced with a compass abbreviation and a short sentence: "Swarm from SW in 180 s: 3 creatures,
  antidote CCF." (SO, NO, O in French).
- No stage needs a sorter any more: stage 2's mechanic is now five creatures shot down by a turret, Sting or Maw,
  the stage 2 swarms, in place of 30 items sorted aside. A game saved on the old count gets the new one.
- A sorter on a nerve has the gate's tools: besides the gene asked, whole or one position, it can sort on a threat,
  no threat, a store low or full and their mixes, by batches, listening to the store the player picks.
- The eyedropper key (A on AZERTY, Q on QWERTY) over bare ground puts down what is in hand, like key 1, instead
  of an error.
- A bridge's card has "Retirer le pont (garder le tapis)": the bridge goes, the belt it was laid over stays and
  keeps running.
