# Strainworks 0.1.18

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## 3D curvature

- The ground is rounded on the sides too, like a small planet.
- A slider sets its strength from 0 to 100 %.
- Labels and lists near the top of the screen no longer vanish on curved ground.

## Mutations

- Mutations land at random on live breaches, sharing rather than piling on one, and spread from the nearest belt
  outward over time.

## The guide

- The Ligase goes two cells right of the core, facing it.
- Belts are short: the guide takes the shortest way and crosses a line with a bridge rather than going round.
