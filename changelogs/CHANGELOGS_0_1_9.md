# Strainworks 0.1.9

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The main screen

- The title comes alive: its letters divide into place, a living gradient and a glow run through them, a
  mutation's flicker crosses them now and then, and a double helix turns under them.
- The planet screen gets a bloom on its lights, a sweep of light, drifting spores, a vignette and fine grain.
- On a phone the finish is lighter, and with reduced motion the title stays still.
