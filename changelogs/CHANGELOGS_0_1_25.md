# Strainworks 0.1.25

Release date: 2026-11-24. It also brings everything listed for 0.1.24, which was not published on its own.

## Buildings

- The Laboratory: built once, it sells upgrades with DNA, each level opening at a stage: drones up to six, drone
  speed, repair, defence reach and sight. New games start with two drones.
- The Inverter: a gene in, the same bases in the opposite order out.
- The Screen: wired to a nerve, it shows the antidote its network asks for.
- A Ligase wired to a nerve empties its inputs when the antidote asked for changes.

## Balance

- Every stage after the first asks twice as much.
- Stage 5 halves the price of the next drone instead of giving one.

## Interface

- The drones panel shows six places, those not bought faded with the next price.
