# Strainworks 0.1.6

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## Music

- Four pieces: the main theme on the planet, two calm pieces in turn during a game, and a fight piece
  while an assault is on, crossfaded.
- The menu has a music volume and a switch, kept for the next visit. The music starts with the first press
  and is left out of the offline cache: the game plays without it offline.

## Lines

- Belts, trails and dismantling are drawn as a ghost showing their cost, and built when the pointer is let
  go. Escape gives the line up.
- A bridge is placed only where a line crosses a belt and goes on straight; a line that ends or turns on a
  belt joins it.
