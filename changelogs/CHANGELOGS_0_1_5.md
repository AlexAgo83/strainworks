# Strainworks 0.1.5

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The first game

- The guide has nine steps. The Ligase is explained, then placed on a spot offered between the harvesters
  and the core; each harvester is then connected to its numbered input, and the Ligase to the core.
- Each step is checked on the game itself: a base that reached its input, a gene the core ate.
- The button to press stands out in the dock and in the open family, with its key, and the rest of the
  dock steps back.
- The belt to draw is traced along the grid. A target out of sight, at the edge of the screen or under the
  interface gets a big arrow that points at it and takes the view there.

## Fixes

- The station picture, shown when the 3D engine cannot load, is drawn from the current model.
- The model lab clears the nest and the drone when another model is shown, and its state field keeps
  every key instead of passing them to the game's shortcuts.
