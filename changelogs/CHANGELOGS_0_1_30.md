# Strainworks 0.1.30

Release date: 2026-11-25.

## Worms

- New games lay worm tracks two cells wide: a maglev way with lit rails, rounded curves and pylons.
- Pockets become 2 by 2 silos that show their level in the colour of what they hold.
- The worm is a levitating armoured train with glass pods lit by their load and a trail behind it.
- Capacities double: 80 a hold, 240 a silo, loading twice as fast.
- The Laboratory sells two more holds at stage 5, up to five per worm.
- Games started before this version keep their one-cell tracks and worms.
