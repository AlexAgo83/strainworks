# Strainworks 0.1.1

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The game

- In English and French. The game opens in English and asks for its language once; the menu changes it,
  and a change reopens the game in progress.
- The 3D curvature is off by default.
- The bottom of the menu shows the game version and opens the repository. A game is exported from its
  hexagon on the planet.

## Fixes

- The statistics sheet keeps its machines in their column, and its close button is centered.
