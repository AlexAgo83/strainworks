# Strainworks 0.1.33

Release date: 2026-11-25.

## Demo

- The demo base is rebuilt the way a player would lay it out, on the real deposits:
  - harvesters on S, N and F feed one mixed bus;
  - C comes from the west field by worm, between two silos on the maglev loop;
  - the antidote factory runs the bus round a Ligase past three sorters on its nerve, and two burners take the rest;
  - the defence line across the creatures' way is fed by splitters: turret, mister, sting and glue;
  - an encapsulator makes the harpoon's magazines, with carbon from the K deposit on its own line.
- No gun is loaded by hand any more: every defence fills from its own line before the first assault.
- The stray test belts that had slipped into the demo are gone.

## Fixes

- A 2 by 2 silo set to unload empties on every free side. Before, it only gave on the north and west of its
  first cell, so a belt on its east or south side stayed empty.
