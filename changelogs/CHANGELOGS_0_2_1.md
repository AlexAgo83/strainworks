# Strainworks 0.2.1

Release date: 2026-11-24.

A fix release: nothing the game asks for at a stage is out of reach any more.

- The nerve and the Assembleur open at stage 2 instead of stage 3. The first defence guide, which fires with the
  first swarm at stage 2, asked for a nerve that was not yet open, and a stage 2 recipe drawn for a new game asked
  for a strand that only the Assembleur makes.
- The texts that say what each stage unlocks now name what really opens: the Veins are no longer announced at
  stage 2, and the isotope, the heavy capsule and the half-price drone are announced by the stage that gives them.
- A new test reads the game's own unlock tables at every stage and checks the drawn and fixed recipes, the stage
  mechanic, both guides, each family's antidote and the unlock texts against them.
