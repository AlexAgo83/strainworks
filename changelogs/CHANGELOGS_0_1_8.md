# Strainworks 0.1.8

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The goal

- A first game opens on a card that tells the goal in three moving pictures made of the game's own models
  and marks: grow the core through five stages, feed it genes, defend it. The menu shows it again.
- The core sheet opens on its five stages, the current one highlighted.

## Sound

- Every action, machine and fight has its sound, in two takes the game alternates, with an Effects volume
  and switch in the menu, at half the music's volume by default.
- Sounds from the map are placed as the player looks: louder near the middle of the screen, fading past its
  edges and when zoomed out, and heard from their side.
- Eight calm pieces and five fight pieces of music.
