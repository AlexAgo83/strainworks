# Strainworks 0.1.12

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The game

- A new game starts on the core alone, the view centred on it; the guide builds the first line.
- Ranks cost two and a half times more. Dismantling a finished building returns half of its price, a site
  given up unfinished all of it.
- A radar shows its wave when built and while selected, and stays still otherwise.
- Over a building just placed, the ghost keeps the way it faces; a tap still turns it.

## Sound and music

- The calm music plays in a new order on every visit and every round.
- Zooming out quiets the map much more, repeating machine and fight sounds are softer, and the DNA sound
  is a soft low blop.
