# Strainworks 0.1.0

Release date: 2026-11-23.

The first playable version.

## The game

- Harvest four bases, carbon and isotope; write genes, splice strands, pack capsules, magazines and heavy capsules.
- Feed the organism through five stages, from Cellule to Noyau.
- Fight breaches with the exact opposite of their gene: three turrets, walls, nests with hit points.
- A nervous system: radar pylons sense a wave, nerves switch gates and sorters to its antidote.
- From stage 4 the creatures mutate: a new family every five minutes, up to eight.
- Builder drones for every site, dismantling and repair; a fourth drone at stage 5.
- Belts, harvesters and cysts in three ranks, bridges, splitters, veins and worms.
- An endless map under fog of war, four kinds of nests, five levels of distance.
- A guided start.

## The interface

- An organic planet with one hexagon a game, export and import.
- A glass interface over a full-screen scene, in 2D and 3D, on desktop and phone.
- A Statistics sheet: production, ADN, defense, threat and what slows the factory.
- Drones in one compact pill, each with its 3D model and its state.
- A small-planet curvature for the 3D view: none, soft or strong.
- Installable, playable offline once loaded.
- A live performance graph in the settings, and a fast 3D quality.
