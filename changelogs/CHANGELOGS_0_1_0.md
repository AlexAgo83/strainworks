# Strainworks 0.1.0

Release date: to be set, publication pending.

The first playable version.

## The game

- Harvest four bases and carbon, write genes, splice strands, pack capsules and magazines.
- Feed the organism through three stages.
- Fight breaches with the exact opposite of their gene: three turrets, walls, nerves that steer gates and sorters.
- Belts, harvesters and cysts in three ranks, bridges, splitters, veins and worms.
- An endless map under fog of war, four kinds of nests, five levels of distance.

## The interface

- An organic planet with one hexagon a game, export and import.
- A grafted interface over a full-screen scene, in 2D and 3D, on desktop and phone.
- Installable, playable offline once loaded.
