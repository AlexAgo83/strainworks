# Strainworks 0.1.4

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## Controls

- W A S D moves the view beside the arrows, Z Q S D on a French keyboard: keys are read by where they sit.
- The eyedropper: Q (A on a French keyboard) takes the building under the pointer in hand, with its heading
  and rank.
- In an open family or the statistics, Shift with a digit takes the machine or the tab.
- A right click held still for 0.6 s dismantles what is under it, with a filling ring. Moved, it still
  moves the view.
- The commands sheet changes the keys of the view, the eyedropper, rotation and pause. A key already taken
  is swapped, and a button brings the defaults back.
