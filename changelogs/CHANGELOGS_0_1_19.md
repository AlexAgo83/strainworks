# Strainworks 0.1.19

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## Recipes

- From stage 2 the core asks several recipes at once, drawn at random for each game: 2 at stage 2, 3 at stages 3
  and 4, 4 at stage 5.
- They use only bases with deposits in the world, never an enemy family, and never repeat in a stage.
- The top bar shows the next recipe to make and how many others wait; the core's sheet lists them all.
- Games in progress keep their recipes.

## Saves

- A game at stage 5 exports and imports again.
