# Strainworks 0.1.17

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## 3D curvature

- The ground bends away with depth, smoothly from the middle of the screen, with no fold.
- Buildings lean with the ground, so lines of belts stay joined.
- The cursor, the grid and the previews follow the curved ground: what you aim at is what you click.

## Starting out

- Every new game opens on its goal.
- The guide traces belts from a harvester's way out, turns harvesters and the Ligase toward the core as they are
  taken, and says that their arrow is their only way out.
- A selected 3x3 core shows its twelve inputs on its faces.
