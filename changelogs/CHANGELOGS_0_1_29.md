# Strainworks 0.1.29

Release date: 2026-11-24.

## Defences

- The Glue (stage 2): a 2 by 2 pool fed with genes. Whatever crosses it slows down and burns a little.
- The Mister (stage 4): a cloud of antidote three cells around it every two seconds, on a whole swarm at once.
- The Harpoon (stage 5): a slow, heavy bolt with a very long reach that runs through a whole file. Capsules only.
- All three have MK2 and MK3.

## Nerves

- The message on a nerve starts from its source, the core or the threat, and shows the bases themselves.
- The Screen is a flat lit table, readable from above.
- A radar watches creatures, nests or both.
- Each nerve network has a colour, picked from the sheet of anything wired to it.
- On a net asking for a strand, sorters and gates read positions 1 to 6, a wired Joiner puts each gene in its
  place, and a Packer on the core's net follows the recipe's box.

## Balance and interface

- MK3 opens at stage 5.
- Trays list buildings in the order they open.

## Fixes

- An arm lifting off a belt no longer crashes the game.
