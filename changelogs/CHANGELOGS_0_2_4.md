# Strainworks 0.2.4

Release date: 2026-11-25.

DNA balance after the automated play-through: the swarms now pay for the defence they call for.

- Stage 2 asks for 3 creatures killed by a gun instead of 5, so the first swarm can be enough. A saved game already
  at stage 2 keeps its progress, capped one kill short of the new count.
- A creature killed pays 3 DNA instead of 1. A swarm of eight to ten creatures pays about one more turret, and the
  core's food still pays nothing.
