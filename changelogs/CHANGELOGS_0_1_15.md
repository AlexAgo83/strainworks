# Strainworks 0.1.15

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The core

- New games stand the core on a 3x3 plinth, fed from any of its twelve faces; its arms climb as a crown over it.
- Games from before keep their one-cell core.

## Machines

- Any machine holding something can be emptied from its sheet.

## Fights

- Nests spasm, flash and spatter when hit, and bleed when badly hurt.
- Creatures flinch when hit, and the dead lie on the ground for 20 seconds before sinking.

## Settings and music

- Back to the planet and Restart are the menu's first rows; pages no longer end on a stray rule.
- The station's hum on the planet follows the music: off, or a piece chosen or stopped, and it goes quiet.
- The playing piece stops on its button, and nothing plays while the game is hidden.
