# Strainworks 0.1.38

Release date: 2026-11-24.

## Demo

- The demo hexagon is back, with a new base built piece by piece the way a player would, then played: a stage 6
  station that runs by itself, fresh every time it opens.
- An antidote factory: three Ligase modules on consoles (CNN, CNF and CFF), each fed by three listening sorters
  on a rank 3 bus. Two Encapsulators send CNN and CFF capsules to the core over the bus by two bridges.
- A second row joins FFC and FNF into FFCFNF strands in an Assembler and boxes them for the core. The first row is
  served first: the second gets what it leaves, plus F straight from the F harvester.
- Defence: the CNF antidote is boxed in magazines for three turrets.
- A north-west outpost faces its nest: an MK3 module makes CCS, the antidote of the nest's SSC family, for three
  turrets, a sting and a maw at the edge of its circle. A large harvester on the guarded F field wakes the nest, and
  the demo opens in the middle of the fight.

## Burner

- The burner has ranks: it clears 4, 8 or 12 items a second (30 and 90 ADN to raise it), and wears its rank ring.

## Bridge

- Each lane of a bridge runs at the pace of its own belts. A slow line crossing a fast bus no longer slows the bus.
  The bridge's card shows both paces.
