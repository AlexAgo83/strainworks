# Strainworks 0.1.37

Release date: 2026-11-24.

## Nerves

- Every listener (gate, sorter, Ligase, assembler, encapsulator, screen) chooses its source on its network: "Listens
  to: the whole network. Change", then tap a gun, a radar, the core, a cyst or a console; tap the piece itself to go
  back to the whole network. A gun or radar gives the antidote of what it sees, the core its recipe, a console its gene
  set by hand, a cyst its level (for gates). One network can run several independent lines.
- A gate on a stock condition with no cyst chosen listens to the nearest one.
- A selected listener shows a dashed line to its source.
- A cyst's threshold reads in items: "Full at: 30 / 60".
- Batches: a gate on a condition can let 5, 10, 20 or 50 items through each time it opens, then waits for the
  condition to drop.
- The gate's card says why it is green or red, which cyst it listens to with its level, and how much of its batch passed.
- The gate's lamp and the cyst's and gate's help texts follow these rules.

## Music

- Ten new pieces, each in its own style: five calm (Abyssal Glow, Lantern Drift, Night Culture, Pollen Clock, Seed
  Waltz) and five for assaults (Breach Protocol, Chitin War, Drums of the Hive, Acid Bloom, Infection). The game now
  has 16 calm pieces and 10 assault pieces.
- In automatic mode no piece comes back before its whole list was played: the fight pieces now go round a shuffled
  list like the calm ones, and a calm piece cut by a fight counts as heard.
- On a phone the playlist scrolls to its last piece: the menu keeps clear of the screen's safe areas.

## Laboratory

- The upgrades sit in tabs (Drones, Defence, Logistics, Recycler), each with its levels bought and a dot when one can
  be bought now; the list scrolls inside the panel, so it stays on screen as upgrades are added.
