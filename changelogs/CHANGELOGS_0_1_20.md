# Strainworks 0.1.20

Release date: 2026-11-23.

## The end of a game

- A sixth stage, Hatching, with five recipes. Meeting them wins the game: a tally of the time, creatures, breaches
  and genes, then free play or the planet.

## Creatures

- One body per leading base: bloat, centipede, beetle, stilt walker. The gene glows on the back; farther nests send
  bigger ones.

## Building

- Lines run straight and turn only on a real turn; a diagonal gives one L. R, or Flip the corner on a phone, lays
  the corner the other way. The piece in hand turns with the line.

## Interface

- The 3D curvature starts at 25 %.
- The core's progress ring sits on its plinth and pulses at each step.
- No text selection, copy bubble or focus ring.
- Help's entries are in Developer.
- Phones: the side controls fold under a tab below the menu button; the drones panel is hidden.

## Sound

- The music steps back while an event sound plays.
