# Strainworks 0.2.5

Release date: 2026-11-25.

- A gun costs the same however many are built: turret 40, Maw 60, Sting 80, mister 120, harpoon 180 DNA. Before,
  each cost half that plus 10 DNA for every gun already built, so a twelfth turret cost 130. A defence is now held
  back by the ammunition lines it needs, not by a climbing price. Walls keep their small step every five, and
  dismantling still returns half.
- The top bar is shorter: the core's hunger is a "½ Faim · 3" pill whose tooltip names the genes, and the swarm
  reads "Essaim 2:54 · NO · 7" with its antidote stock ("0 antidote") instead of the arrow and the sentence.
